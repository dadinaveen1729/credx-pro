How It All Began

A Dream Built
on Belief

Every great story starts with a question: what if? Ours started with a bigger one — what if beauty felt like belonging?

VIC Salon was not built overnight. It was shaped by years of passion, hundreds of conversations with clients who deserved more, and a profound belief that a visit to the salon should leave you not just looking beautiful — but feeling truly seen.

Today, from our home in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, VIC Salon stands as a destination where artistry meets warmth — where every guest is treated not as a client, but as family.

The Founders

The Heartbeat
Behind VIC

Two passionate souls. One shared vision. An unwavering commitment to you.

M
Co-Founder & Creative Director

Madhuri
Bollaram

She grew up watching her mother spend hours in front of a mirror, trying to feel beautiful in a world that rarely made space for her. That image never left Madhuri. It became her why — the deep, burning reason she chose beauty as her life's calling.

With years of training under master stylists and hands that understood the language of hair before words could express it, Madhuri built a reputation for transformations that went beyond the surface. Her consultations are conversations. Her treatments are rituals. Her clients leave not just looking different — they leave feeling rediscovered.

When she co-founded VIC Salon, she brought with her every lesson, every tear, every moment of wonder she had witnessed in the chair. She designed every detail of the VIC experience — from the moment you walk in, to the way the air smells, to the exact tone of warmth you are greeted with — so that no guest ever feels ordinary.

"I never wanted to run a salon," she often says. "I wanted to create a feeling. And I wanted that feeling to belong to every woman who walked through our door."

"Beauty is not about changing who you are. It is about revealing who you always were."
— Madhuri Bollaram, Co-Founder
B
Co-Founder & Wellness Director

Bhaghat
G

Some people find their purpose through ambition. Bhaghat found his through empathy. As someone who had seen how a great spa treatment could restore not just the body but a person's entire sense of self — he knew that wellness was far more powerful than people gave it credit for.

Armed with deep expertise in wellness and spa therapies, and an instinct for creating spaces where people genuinely exhale, Bhaghat became the architect of VIC's philosophy of holistic care. The therapists he trained, the rituals he designed, the quiet luxury he curated — all of it comes from his foundational belief: that every person who closes their eyes in our spa chair deserves to feel completely safe, completely cared for, completely human.

He built VIC not just as a business, but as a mission — to bring world-class wellness experiences to Hyderabad in a way that felt personal, not corporate. To create a space where people come when they are stressed, sad, celebrating, or simply in need of being held in care.

"A salon should give you back something you didn't even know you had lost," he believes. For Bhaghat, VIC Salon does exactly that — every single day.

"When someone trusts you with their face, their hair, their body — they are trusting you with their confidence. That is sacred. We never forget that."
— Bhaghat G, Co-Founder

Together, Madhuri and Bhaghat built something rare — a salon where the people who work there love what they do, and the guests who come there feel it immediately. VIC Salon is their love letter to Hyderabad. And it is crafted, always, for you.

Our
Philosophy

✦

Craftsmanship

We believe beauty is a discipline. Every technique is mastered, every product is chosen with intention, and every treatment is delivered with unwavering precision.

♡

Human Connection

Before any service begins, we listen. We learn your story. We understand your world. Because the best beauty is the kind that understands you.
